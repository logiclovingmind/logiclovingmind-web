Why real estate only

A generic assistant learns generic conversation.

This one was built around how property is actually sold in India: inventory, RERA projects, budgets, possession timelines, and getting someone to the site.

What specialization actually changes.

QUALIFICATIONIt asks for budget band, possession timeline, configuration and location preference, in the order a good salesperson would, and it stops when it has enough.
INVENTORYIt answers on real units — tower, floor, facing, carpet area — instead of sending a brochure and hoping.
RERA PROJECTSMultiple projects, each with its own inventory, pricing and approvals, held separately so a buyer never gets the wrong project's numbers.
SITE VISITSThe close of a property conversation is a visit, not a form fill. The system proposes slots, confirms, and puts it on the calendar.
BUYER BEHAVIOURProperty buyers go quiet for days, return at midnight, and ask the same three questions. The system is built for that rhythm.
HANDOFFYour team opens a lead that already has a budget, a timeline and a booked visit. Nobody starts from “hi.”
