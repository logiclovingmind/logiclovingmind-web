# CLAUDE.md — Logic Loving Mind Website

Operating instructions for building and maintaining the Logic Loving Mind marketing site.
Read this file **and** `design.md` before writing any code. `design.md` governs everything visual;
this file governs content truth, structure, stack, and quality gates. Where they overlap, `design.md` wins on
appearance, this file wins on facts.

---

## 1. The one-line brief

Logic Loving Mind builds intelligent operating systems that run customer conversations for real estate
companies. The site must read like it was built by a small engineering studio for a specialist firm —
exclusive, precise, quietly confident. Not an AI agency landing page.

**The site's single job:** get a qualified real estate operator to either book a demo, apply for Automation
Forever, or call sales — and to believe, before they click, that this was built by people who actually
engineer things.

---

## 2. Non-negotiables

1. **Chrome is monochrome.** Navigation, buttons, forms, footers, tables, borders, and all body text are
   white/grey on near-black. Color enters only through hero backgrounds and product-tier contexts (see
   `design.md` §2).
2. **Three tier hues, never mixed.** IZI = fiery red. EON = electric blue. OMNI = violet. One hue per
   viewport, maximum. Never two tier colors in the same visual field except in the one deliberate
   three-way comparison moment.
3. **The logo is never recolored.** Both SVGs use `currentColor`. They inherit white, or grey when subdued.
   Never tier-tinted, never gradient-filled, never on a busy background.
4. **No invented proof.** No fake logos, fake testimonials, fake customer counts, fake awards, no
   "trusted by 500+ companies." The company has three pilot seats and two founders. That scarcity *is*
   the story — tell it honestly.
5. **Every number on the site comes from §4 of this file.** If a number isn't there, don't put it on the page.
6. **Anti-slop clauses in `design.md` §9 are hard rules, not preferences.** A build that violates them
   fails review regardless of how polished it looks.

## 3. Audience and voice

**Reader:** a real estate developer, builder, or sales-company owner in India. Runs a sales team.
Gets 100–1,000+ WhatsApp leads a month. Loses money to slow replies. Has been pitched by three
"AI automation agencies" already and didn't buy. Skeptical, numerate, time-poor, reads on a phone.

**Voice rules**

- Declarative and short. Sentence case. No exclamation marks anywhere on the site.
- Numbers and mechanisms instead of adjectives. "Replies in under five seconds at 2 a.m." beats
  "lightning-fast responses."
- Say what the system *does*, not what it will do *for your business*. The reader does that math.
- The negative positioning is on-brand and should appear early: not a chatbot, not a marketing agency,
  not an automation agency, not an AI wrapper.
- Never claim the system replaces the sales team. It removes repetitive conversation work and hands
  humans warm, qualified, structured leads.
- Buttons name their outcome and keep that name through the flow. "Book a demo" → confirmation says
  "Demo booked."
- Errors state what happened and what to do. They don't apologize and they aren't vague.
- Empty and loading states are instructions, not moods.

**Banned words and constructions** (instant rewrite): revolutionize, unlock, supercharge, transform,
elevate, empower, seamless / seamlessly, cutting-edge, game-changing, next-level, harness the power of,
leverage AI to, in today's fast-paced world, unleash, journey, solutions (as a noun for the product),
"we're on a mission to," "imagine a world where," any headline built as "AI-Powered [Noun] for [Noun]."
No emoji. No sparkle, rocket, robot, or brain glyphs, in copy or as icons.

## 5. Conversion architecture

Three actions, ranked. Every page states exactly one primary action.

| Rank | Action | Destination | Where it's primary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Book a demo** | `/book-demo` + WhatsApp deep link | Home, Products, IZI, EON, How It Works, Why Real Estate Only |
| 2 | **Apply for Automation Forever** | `/automation-forever` | Automation Forever page; secondary in home |
| 3 | **Contact sales** | tel / `/contact` | OMNI, About, Contact |

Rules: one primary CTA per viewport. The persistent header holds a single primary action ("Book a demo")
and nothing else. The WhatsApp demo link is the highest-intent path on the site — treat it as a first-class
action, not a footer link, because it puts the visitor inside the product in one tap.

Eight top-level routes, three product children.


### `/` Home

1. **Hero** — the signature canvas (`design.md` §6). Headline states what the system does, not what AI is.
   Primary: Book a demo. Secondary: the WhatsApp live demo link.
2. **Negative positioning** — the four "is not" lines, set as type. This is the second thing a skeptic reads.
3. **What the system does** — capability list from §4. Not six equal icon cards; use an asymmetric,
   type-led layout with the mono spec treatment.
4. **The progression** — Fire → Electricity → Intelligent Systems, dated. The one place all three tier hues
   may coexist, as three separated fields.
5. **Three tiers** — IZI / EON / OMNI, each in its own hue, capacity in mono figures, price visible.
6. **Ownership** — you own the accounts, you pay Meta and tokens directly, no markup.
7. **Automation Forever** — 3 seats, the refund condition stated plainly.
8. **Close** — Book a demo.

### `/products`

Overview + one honest comparison table. Rows are the real feature and capacity lines from §4.
Column headers carry tier hue as a hairline top rule and mono label only; cells stay monochrome.
No "most popular" badge.

### `/products/[tier]`

Per-tier template: hue-tinted hero field · name etymology and brand meaning (this is the differentiator —
give it real space) · what it includes · capacity spec strip in mono · pricing stated in figures ·
who it's for · CTA. OMNI's CTA is Contact sales; pricing reads "Custom."

### `/automation-forever`

Scarcity stated first (3 seats). Then the deposit mechanic and the refund condition as a clear,

### `/how-it-works`

Deployment sequence — this content genuinely *is* ordered, so sequence markers are earned here and

End on the no-markup statement.

### `/why-real-estate-only`

The strongest argument on the site. A generic assistant learns generic conversation; this one is built
around real estate workflows, buyer behavior, qualification logic, and site-visit booking. Use concrete

budget and possession-timeline qualification.

### `/about`

Philosophy (the invention progression), the company's position, the two founders and their scopes,
MAYA introduced as internal intelligence, HSR Layout Bangalore.

### `/contact`

Phone, both emails, WhatsApp demo, location. Form with named fields.
Every channel says what it's for and how fast it answers.

### `/book-demo`

Two paths, side by side, equal weight: **instant** — WhatsApp demo with MAYA, one tap;

## 7. Stack and structure

**Preferred:** Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind, statically generated. If the environment
can't run a framework, build multi-page static HTML + Tailwind with a shared header/footer partial —
the route map and design tokens are identical either way. Do not introduce a UI component library, a
CSS-in-JS runtime, an animation library beyond what the hero needs, or an icon pack.

```
/app or /pages          One directory per route above
/components
  /layout               Header, Footer, Shell, SectionRule
  /brand                Mark.tsx, Wordmark.tsx  (inline SVG, currentColor)
  /hero                 Continuum.tsx + fallback still
  /ui                   Button, Field, SpecStrip, ComparisonTable, TierPanel
/content                One typed data file per tier + program constants
/styles                 tokens.css (design.md §1–§4 verbatim), globals.css
/public                 mark.svg, wordmark.svg, favicons, og images, fonts
```

**Content lives in typed data, not JSX.** `content/tiers.ts` exports IZI/EON/OMNI objects with
`price`, `support`, `includes[]`, `capacity{users, conversationsPerDay, messagesPerDay}`, `hue`,
`etymology`, `brandMessage`. Pages map over it. A price change is a one-line edit.

**Tokens are CSS custom properties**, defined once, consumed everywhere. No hardcoded hex in components.
No arbitrary Tailwind values for color or spacing — extend the theme from the tokens instead.

## 8. Brand asset handling

- `mark.svg` — viewBox `237.685 221.451 552.557 556.337`, near-square, angular M/shield form with two
  peaks and a converging base point. Inline it; size with `width`/`height`; color with `color`.
- `wordmark.svg` — viewBox `56.985 387 1177.887 56.848`, aspect ratio **≈20.7:1**. It needs horizontal room.
  Minimum render width **160px**; below that use the mark alone. Never stack, condense, or letterspace it.
- Header lockup: mark + wordmark, gap = 0.4× mark height, vertically centered on the wordmark's cap height.
- Clear space around either asset: **0.5× mark height** on all sides. Nothing crosses it.
- Favicon and OG mark: derived from `mark.svg` only, white on `--void`.
- The mark may be used oversized, cropped, and low-contrast as a background structural element —
  at ≤4% opacity, monochrome, never tier-tinted, never rotated, never as a repeating pattern.

- Real estate terms spelled the way Indian operators search them: RERA, site visit, possession,
  builder, developer, lakh. Keep ₹ figures in text, not images.

## 10. Performance and quality gates

A build ships only if all of these pass.

- **LCP < 1.8s** on simulated 4G / mid-tier Android. **CLS < 0.02**. **INP < 150ms**.
- App JS ≤ **150KB gzipped** excluding the hero canvas. The hero canvas is a **dynamic import that loads
  after LCP** and never blocks first paint; a static still renders in its place immediately.
- Hero canvas: cap DPR at 1.5 · pause via `IntersectionObserver` when out of view · pause on
  `visibilitychange` · hard-disable and show the still on `prefers-reduced-motion`, on
  `navigator.hardwareConcurrency <= 4`, on WebGL failure, and at viewports under 768px.
- Fonts self-hosted `woff2`, subset to Latin, `font-display: swap`, with `size-adjust` fallback metrics
  so swap causes no reflow. Max 4 font files total.
- Zero raster images unless they are real product screenshots. No stock photography. No icon font.
- **WCAG 2.2 AA.** 4.5:1 for text — tier *core* hues never carry text, only the glow variants
  (`design.md` §2). Visible `:focus-visible` on every interactive element. Full keyboard path through
  nav, forms, and the mobile menu. Labels are real `<label>`s, never placeholder-only.
- Responsive and correct at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1440, 1920. No horizontal scroll at 360.
- Lighthouse ≥ 95 on Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, SEO.
